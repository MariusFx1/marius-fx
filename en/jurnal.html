Personal journal

Your trading journal

A journal shows you what you actually do, not what you think you do. By logging every entry you build discipline, learn from mistakes before they repeat and check whether you followed your plan.

Want to practise without real money first? In the backtesting simulator you trade on real historical charts, and you can send your closed trades here, tagged "Simulator".

Your trades are saved only in this browser, on your device. Nobody else can see them.

If you clear your browser data, use incognito mode or change phones, the journal is lost. Every now and then, use Export backup and keep the file.

Your browser doesn't allow local storage (you may be in private mode). You can fill in the journal, but it will be lost when you close the page, so export it first.

    Guide

    What to log for every trade

    1. The plan: the pair, direction, entry, stop loss and take profit, before you press the button.
    2. The reason: why you entered (the setup). If you can't write it in one sentence, don't enter.
    3. The risk: the lot size and what % of your account you're risking. Ideally 1–2%.
    4. The result in R: how many "risks" you won or lost, not just the money.
    5. Discipline: did you follow your plan? Answer honestly.
    6. Emotions and the lesson: what you felt and what you'll do differently next time.

    Before you enter

    Checklist before entering a trade

    • Do I have a clear plan for this entry?
    • Is the stop loss set?
    • Risk ≤ 1–2% of the account?
    • R:R ≥ 1:2?
    • Am I free of emotional trading (fear, FOMO, revenge)?

    A single "no" = you don't enter. The market always offers another opportunity.
