01 · The foundation

Basic trading rules

Ten simple principles that separate disciplined trading from gambling. Read them before every session.

  1. 01

    Risk a maximum of 1–2% per trade

    Never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single position. That's how you survive a losing streak, too.

  2. 02

    Always use a stop loss

    Every trade has a stop loss set in advance. Don't move it further away when the price goes against you.

  3. 03

    Have a written trading plan

    Define in writing what you trade, when you enter, when you exit and how much you risk. Without a plan, decisions become impulsive.

  4. 04

    Respect the risk/reward ratio

    Look for trades with a ratio of at least 1:2: the potential gain should be at least twice the risk you take.

  5. 05

    Keep a trading journal

    Log every entry, the reason, the result and your emotional state. The journal shows you what really works.

  6. 06

    Beware of leverage

    Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. High leverage can empty an account very quickly.

  7. 07

    No revenge trading

    After a loss, take a break. Don't try to "win it back" right away: emotions lead to bad decisions.

  8. 08

    Be careful around major news

    Major economic announcements (interest rates, NFP, inflation) can cause sudden moves and slippage.

  9. 09

    Start on a demo account

    Practise your strategy on a demo account until you're consistent. Only then move to small amounts of real money.

  10. 10

    Protect your capital, above all

    Your first job isn't to win, it's not to lose your capital. Without capital, there are no more trades.
