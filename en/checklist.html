Pre-trade checklist

Tick what you see on the chart and get a live score: how well the setup follows the rules of a swing trading plan. Enter only when the zone, confirmation, risk and R:R are all in order.

1. Analysis timeframeOn which timeframe did you find the zone and structure?
0/9

For swing trading, read zones and trend on W1, D1 or H4. Low timeframes are full of noise.

2. Support / resistance zoneIs price at a clear support/resistance (or supply/demand) zone?
0/15
3. Candle confirmationWhich candle formation do you see at the zone?
0/12
4. Chart pattern / setupWhich structure do you see on your working timeframe?
0/12
5. Trend aligned with the higher timeframeIs the trade direction the same as the D1/W1 trend?
5. Confirmed reversal (Head and Shoulders)Is the prior trend exhausted (structure broken), and has the neckline been broken + retested?
0/12

Head and Shoulders is a reversal pattern: you enter against the prior trend, so you are not looking for alignment with it. You want proof that it has ended: broken structure, and a neckline that has been broken and retested.

6. Retest + confirmationHas price retested the zone and has the confirmation formed (closed candle)?
0/12
7. Confirmation / entry timeframeOn which timeframe did the entry confirmation form?
0/8

The ideal confirmation comes on H4 (or D1). Very low timeframes produce many false signals.

8. Risk 1–3% of the accountWhat percentage of your account are you risking on this trade?
0/10

Maximum 3% per trade, ideally 1–2%. Work out the exact lot size in the lot size calculator.

9. Risk/Reward at least 1:2Enter SL and TP in pips, or the R:R directly.
0/10

Marius's rule: only enter if the TP gives you at least 2R, with the SL placed behind structure.
